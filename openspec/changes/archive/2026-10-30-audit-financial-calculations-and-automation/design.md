# Design

## Context

The audit uses cached `origin/main` at `b255833` and Android companion `7468256`; the working checkout is older and contains unrelated changes. The TECNO screen was inspected only for static labels. No web figures or personal amounts were compared, so the exact discrepancy is not established. See proposal.md.

`/pulse` and dashboard overview have different daily allowance formulae, and the latter mixes the selected timeframe's outflow with current-cycle remaining days. The mobile home counts recorded local notification rows using device-local notification dates, rather than canonical ledger rows. The emergency calculation sums primary-category spending over the last 30 days plus obligation minimums, defaults unclassified categories to primary, admits future-dated entries, and can mark missing expenses healthy. Its fallback balance differs between dashboard and net-worth callers. Kakeibo currently groups five classifications into three benchmark cards. Its savings calculation combines saving-classified expenses with qualified paired movements; audit all movement creation paths and prevent overlaps before interpreting it as savings.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:** Trace each displayed number to ledger entries and explicit policy; retain historical evidence; make unknowns visible; coordinate backend, web and Android compatibility.

**Non-Goals:** Financial advice, automatic bank transfers, replacing actual bank balances, inventing future salary dates, silently changing transaction history, or accessing production for investigation.

## Decisions

### Shared reporting and periods
Define one server-side reporting contract with amount, period, timezone, gross/net reimbursement treatment, included sources, transfer/fee treatment and drill-down. Calendar periods use application timezone; current periods end at now, historical periods at their true boundary. Android fetches the same summary as web; show last successful update and offline status, never merge local notifications into server totals. Local notification activity remains separately labelled. Savings, net cash flow, and cash balance are separate concepts. Consolidate or retire legacy calculation paths and do not convert null/unknown to zero.

### Salary cycles
An owner configures and confirms an identifying salary source; incoming matching candidates are distinguishable from bonuses and refunds, and only confirmed qualifying salary receipts open a cycle. When a payment from the same employer could be salary or a bonus, ask the owner to confirm it (owner decision 2026-10-30). Provide owner confirmation/correction of ambiguous candidates and manual selection of existing income, not fabricated transactions. End a historical cycle at the next confirmed receipt; leave the current end unknown. Dates can be corrected, including duplicate same-day receipts, deleted or edited salary transactions, and historical gaps. Keep calendar reports available before the first confirmed salary; never use the old fixed day as a silently asserted fact. A candidate's exact matching and bonus handling must be explicit and tested against false matches.

For the first implementation, the owner selects an existing income as the source example. Match its normalized merchant when available, otherwise its normalized description; show the chosen field and text so it can be reviewed. Exact matching may miss descriptions that change and is not by itself proof of salary. On 2026-10-30 the owner approved automatic confirmation when a payment matches the confirmed source, explicitly says `gaji` or `salary`, and does not indicate a bonus or refund. Other source matches require review; allow explicit selection of any eligible existing income when matching misses it. Require a single active receipt per local calendar date and send same-day collisions to review; reject reimbursements, future-dated entries, internal movements and other users' transactions. Changing or deleting the linked transaction changes whether it is eligible without creating or deleting any unrelated ledger entry. Source selection and receipt confirmation/revocation have separate audit events that contain identifiers and actions, not raw financial data.

### Essential cost and coverage — deferred
On 2026-10-30 the owner requested waiting another two to three months of use before revisiting the essential-cost estimate and accepted that the current calculation may be inaccurate in the meantime. Preserve the existing cost, reserve and coverage calculation and presentation for this change, including existing fallback behavior. Do not treat the passage of time or the presence of transactions as proof that expense history is complete. Revisit history completeness, unusual expenses, debt overlap, designated reserves and unavailable-input behavior in a later decision. A six-month median was researched as a possible heuristic, not approved for implementation in this change.

### Observed cash flow and planning
Remove linear projections, daily burn averages and judgments as primary insights, and remove a daily safe-to-spend number derived from an assumed payday. Show observed inflow, net outflow, net cash flow, budget remaining where configured, and known unpaid obligations with dates. Plans are expectations, not payments or guarantees; provide period and data-quality context. Keep category comparisons and an optional descriptive chart without interpreting a no-spend day as a target failure.

### Kakeibo and savings
Display need, want, culture, unexpected and saving individually, with explicit calculation basis and ledger links. Treat 50/30/20 as an optional benchmark with explained group mapping, not a health verdict. Audit the existing expense-plus-movement formula, reverse movements, duplicate matching, goal-linked accounts, fees, investment trades and reimbursements; count fresh savings once, differentiate surplus from savings and retain a traceable explanation. Missing or ambiguous classifications remain reviewable. The current reimbursement service assigns an entire category offset to the category default pillar, even when transaction-level pillars differ; Kakeibo totals can therefore exceed net spending. Split each category's applied reimbursement proportionally across its actual effective transaction pillars, using integer largest-remainder allocation and deterministic tie-breaking to preserve exact totals (owner decision 2026-10-30). This is an allocation across a category, not proof of which individual expense was reimbursed.

### Plans versus actuals
Disable scheduled auto-posting and retire payroll bulk execution and the specialized bill-payment shortcut after migration. Convert existing rules non-destructively into non-posting expectations, keep occurrence history, and offer clear manual recording where capture is unavailable. Matching a notification or manual transaction to an obligation changes its progress once, without adding another transaction. No silent fuzzy match: ambiguous candidates go to review. Preserve investment and debt workflows and their invariant-specific behavior; any confirmed cash transaction is still eligible for explicit recording.

## Implementation decisions (2026-10-30)

Owner decisions recorded on 2026-10-30: salary cycles start only from a confirmed source (the owner selects an existing income as the example); auto-confirm only when a payment matches the source, says `gaji` or `salary`, and has no bonus or refund indicator; ambiguous source matches go to review; the current cycle end stays unknown; reimbursements split across a category's effective pillars by largest remainder with deterministic ties; the payoff-time estimate is removed; emergency cost and coverage stay unchanged for two to three months; linear forecasts, daily averages, pace judgments and the assumed-payday safe-to-spend figure are retired; automatic recurring posting, payroll execution and the bill-payment shortcut are retired (BREAKING) with rules converted to non-posting expectations, actual payments linked without silent fuzzy matching, and manual recording kept for missed notifications; all five Kakeibo classifications are shown with 50/30/20 as an optional reference.

1. **Cycle boundary is the receipt instant.** A confirmed receipt starts the cycle at its recorded timestamp, not at local midnight; spending earlier that day belongs to the previous cycle in every consumer (pulse, overview, analytics, ledger). Rejected: snapping to the local day start, because a same-day expense before the salary arrived would move between cycles when the receipt is corrected.
2. **No confirmed cycle falls back to a labelled calendar month.** `cycle` windows without a receipt use the calendar month with the label "… · siklus gaji belum tersedia" (or "perlu ditinjau" for same-day collisions). Rejected: keeping the fixed payday as a silent fallback.
3. **Old payday setting.** `payday_day` is still accepted and stored for older clients and to date legacy plans that used the payday schedule, but responses carry `Deprecation: true` and a `payday_day_deprecated` notice, and it never creates a cycle boundary. Rejected: 410 on settings updates, because an older page saving another setting would lose that change.
4. **Legacy forecast fields.** Response fields are kept for compatibility: `safe_to_spend_today`, `pace_status`, `spend_velocity_*`, `projected_cycle_outflow`, `cycle_days_*` and `estimated_payoff_months` are `null`. `burn_rate.projected_30d_outflow` keeps its value because it was always the observed trailing 30 days (the report-periods scenario requires it); `trailing_30d_outflow` names it honestly. No web view presents either as a forecast.
5. **Commitments use an explicit 30-day horizon.** Overview returns `upcoming_commitments` (active expense plans due within 30 days, including overdue ones) because an open cycle has no end to bound them. The legacy `upcoming_recurring_expense` now carries that 30-day total. Budget remaining is shown only for month and salary-cycle windows.
6. **Expectations reuse `recurring_rules`.** Migration V37 keeps every rule and occurrence, stores the previous `auto_post` in `legacy_auto_post`, sets `auto_post = false`, and adds `CHECK (auto_post = false)`. Occurrences gain `linked` and `skipped` states; a partial unique index lets one transaction satisfy at most one occurrence. Rejected: a new expectations table with copied data, which would need a second source of truth and a back-mapping for rollback.
7. **Retired endpoints answer 410.** `POST /recurring/execute`, `/recurring/process-due` and `/recurring/payroll/execute` return 410 with `scheduled_posting_retired` or `payroll_execution_retired`. Creating or updating a plan with `auto_post`, `is_payroll_allocation = true` or a new `payday` schedule returns 422 with a code. The background scheduler loop is removed.
8. **Occurrence actions are explicit.** `POST /recurring/{id}/occurrences/link` links a recorded transaction to the next occurrence (or relinks one whose transaction was deleted), `…/skip` records that it did not happen, and `…/record` records one missed occurrence only after the owner has ruled out same-amount captures in the ±7-day window (`possible_duplicate` otherwise). Plans with history cannot be deleted (`expectation_has_history`); deactivation keeps the history. Investment monthly confirmations use `…/record`, keeping the occurrence checks (next due date, not in the future, idempotent per occurrence) and the existing top-up and WAC behaviour.
9. **Debt linking.** `GET /obligations/{id}/payment-candidates` lists unlinked ordinary expenses from the last 45 days, exact minimum-payment amounts first; `POST /obligations/{id}/payments` links one through `transaction_obligation_allocations`, once, refusing an expense linked elsewhere or larger than the remaining balance. Editing or deleting the expense uses the existing allocation reversal, so archive and reactivation are unchanged. Nothing links automatically; one exact candidate is labelled `single`, several are `ambiguous`.
10. **Explicit records keep plan provenance.** An owner-requested record of a missed occurrence keeps `recurring_rule_id` and is written as recurring, reviewed, system, as before. Rejected: relabelling it manual, which would change the investment confirmation contract.
11. **Fresh savings.** The audit found no double count: trades carry no pillar, top-ups and moves between ordinary and savings or investment accounts carry `saving` on the outbound leg only, operational transfers carry none, fee rows are ordinary expenses. Expense pillars sum exactly to net reportable spending (regression test `test_kakeibo_reconciliation.py`).

### Salary marker check against production (2026-10-30)

With the owner's approval, a read-only aggregate query checked production. It returned only yes/no flags, with no names, amounts or dates. The owner's most recent salary notification contains `gaji`/`salary` in both the ledger row and the notification text, so automatic confirmation applies to the current format. For an earlier month the check could not confirm the marker, so salaries without it remain owner-reviewed.

## Open decisions for owner

- **Unused web files.** Resolved 2026-10-30 on the owner's go-ahead: `DailyBudgetBar`, `KakeiboPillarCards`, `KpiRibbon`, `PayrollAllocationModal` (with its test) and the unused `isKakeiboHealthy`/`kakeiboStatusLabel` helpers were removed. The legacy `kakeibo_status`/`want_status` response fields stay for compatibility, per the Kakeibo delta, and no screen reads them.
- **Legacy payday-scheduled plans.** Existing plans with `schedule_type = payday` keep dates derived from the old `payday_day` and are labelled as estimates; new ones cannot be created. Converting them to a fixed day or to "after the next confirmed salary" needs an owner choice.
- **Remaining fixed-day helper.** `get_cycle_window` and the fixed-day branch of `get_timeframe_window` remain only for callers without a user; no HTTP route uses them. Remove them with the payday column in a later change.
- **Auto-linking a single exact candidate.** Kept manual (conservative). The owner may later approve automatic linking of one exact, same-account candidate.

## Rollout and rollback

1. Deploy the backend through the approved pipeline. Flyway applies V35–V37 before the API starts; V37 converts rules in one transaction and is repeatable.
2. Check after deploy: `SELECT COUNT(*) FROM recurring_rules WHERE auto_post` is 0; counts of `recurring_executions` and transactions with `recurring_rule_id` match the pre-deploy counts; due plans appear on Beranda without balance changes.
3. Then install the Android companion 2.2.0, which reads `GET /api/dashboard/cash-flow-summary`. An older companion keeps working because no endpoint it uses changed.
4. Rollback: redeploy the previous release. The schema stays (columns and statuses are additive), so the old code runs, but its scheduler finds no `auto_post` rules and its create path cannot set `auto_post` because of `chk_recurring_rules_non_posting`; old payroll and execute buttons would post again only on explicit owner action. **Guard against silently re-enabling auto-posting:** restoring automatic posting requires a deliberate, reviewed migration that drops `chk_recurring_rules_non_posting` and restores `auto_post` from `legacy_auto_post`, only after reconciling occurrences that were linked, skipped or recorded manually while posting was off; no rollback step does this automatically. Old code does not know the `linked` and `skipped` states; it would only re-execute such an occurrence through an explicit owner action for that exact date, and next due dates have already advanced past them.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- [Cross-device lag or incomplete capture] → explicit freshness, pending/review states and drill-down; never call a device cache the ledger.
- [Existing emergency-fund figures can be inaccurate] → owner accepts the current behavior temporarily; revisit after another two to three months of use.
- [Salary source matches a bonus or duplicate] → classify/correct candidates and preserve an audit trail; no guessed cycle boundary.
- [Turning off posting disrupts existing schedules or breaks older clients] → inventory active rules and clients, staged non-destructive migration and explicit compatibility errors; no automatic deletion.
- [Overlapping reserves and expense/obligation double counting] → retain as known issues for the deferred emergency-fund review.
- [Spec baseline differs from checkout] → rebase this plan against actual merged target and full current specs before implementation; do not overwrite unrelated working-tree changes.

## Migration Plan

Build additive schema and read contracts first, retain existing records and old settings, and validate on synthetic data. Migrate rules to non-posting expectations with auditable mapping; prevent both old and new schedulers from posting during cutover. Ship compatible server and web first, then Android, with versioned or explicitly deprecated endpoints. Reconcile counts, balances and links before and after migration; test interruption and retries. Rollback restores previous code and compatible schema with preserved records; do not automatically resume legacy auto-posting without deliberate owner action and reconciliation.

## Merged-baseline specification conflicts

The following current main requirements must be reconciled as complete delta requirements, preserving unrelated behavior and historical data before implementation. The initial proposal's delta list is incomplete and OpenSpec's syntactic validation does not detect these semantic conflicts.

- `clean-core-ledger`: fixed payday window and clamping, daily allowance and payday settings; retain transfer, authentication, existing emergency runway, current spending and liquid-balance invariants.
- `tactile-pulse-ui`: daily allowance, pace indicator and quick-capture refresh of allowance; retain quick capture, navigation, accessibility and activity refresh.
- `currency-conversion-and-settings`: fixed payday setting and refresh of safe-to-spend; retain profile, currency conversion, emergency multiplier, budgets and validation.
- `reimbursement-offsets`: pulse and emergency runway currently specified gross and safe-to-spend net; preserve reimbursement attribution, excess and unallocated protections and keep the existing emergency cost treatment until its deferred review.
- `kakeibo-decoupling`: references retired safe-to-spend while retaining the shared effective-pillar rule.
- `kazz-ui-and-kakeibo`: three-pillar vocabulary and warning status conflict with five individually visible classifications and optional non-health benchmark; preserve unrelated narrative, keypad and navigation.
- `kakeibo-reconciliation`: benchmark and status requirements, including healthy on an empty cycle, conflict with the no-health-verdict rule; retain five-pillar amounts, defensible denominators and traceable savings.
- `mobile-native-layout`: mobile web currently requires payroll allocation and a three-group pace bar; retain the rest of the mobile home, capture and ledger contract.
- `obligations-debt-tracker`: recurring amortization creates transactions; retain atomic existing-transaction linking, split allocations, correction and reversal.
- `investment-topups`: monthly rule shortcut and occurrence-specific protections must map to non-posting plans while retaining atomic contribution, valuation and history behavior.
- `transaction-ledger-management` and `transaction-provenance`: preserve historical recurring and payroll origin records and labels even after retiring new execution; update present-tense execution scenarios and cycle summary semantics.
- `accessible-financial-input-forms`, `indonesian-daily-finance-ui`, and `account-reconciliation`: remove obsolete payroll/allowance references without weakening accessibility, language or reconciliation protections.
- `report-periods-and-drilldown`: retain calendar selection, filtering and traceability while interpreting cycles as confirmed receipt boundaries and allowing unavailable gaps.
- `mobile-notification-completion`: retain non-destructive companion upgrades, safe identity and privacy; the new summary must not overwrite or erase notification history.
- `finance-dashboard-cockpit` executive ribbon and `clean-core-ledger` net-worth contracts must distinguish liquid funds, investment assets, total assets and liabilities; the existing `/pulse` field named `total_net_worth` is total account balance and does not subtract debt, whereas `/dashboard/net-worth` subtracts active obligations. The shared contract SHALL not label total assets as net worth. Preserve emergency coverage computation separately.

## Reporting inventory at b255833

- Web home `/dashboard/overview` and Analisis `/dashboard/analytics` use `report_windows.py` for local-calendar-day windows converted to UTC; the current cycle still uses the fixed payday. `/dashboard/net-worth` is consumed by accounts. Ledger `/transactions` applies the same period helper and a separate filter-sensitive offset policy.
- `report_figures.py` defines reportable income and expense by category metadata and category names. Expenses without a category are included; reimbursements are not reportable income. Internal movement and investment exclusion depends on reportable category classification, making incorrectly classified records a review concern.
- `reimbursements.py` caps offsets within the same window and category, exposes unapplied and excess amounts, and does not carry excess to another window. Ledger summaries deliberately remain gross when other filters make their scope unsuitable for the standard offset. The shared contract must retain or explicitly revise those visible semantics, with regression coverage.
- `pulse.py` has a separate living-spending definition that excludes saving-pillar expenses, while the reportable expense definition includes reportable saving-pillar expenses. `/pulse` and overview compute daily allowance differently. These are different measures and must not be equated without labels and aligned scope.
- The Android home derives today and recent figures from local notification result rows and device-local notification timestamps; its network client already authenticates to the backend for ingestion and transaction editing. Shared summary delivery must use an authorized server endpoint and include freshness and scope, while retaining notification status separately.
- Required synthetic regressions include mixed transaction pillars in one reimbursement category, partial and excess reimbursements, period cutoffs in application timezone, same-day salary receipts, a transfer with fee, uncategorized entries, filtered ledger gross outflow, offline mobile cache and notifications from multiple devices.
- Account balances use account-level own balances to avoid parent/child double counts; the net-worth endpoint sums top-level aggregate balances and subtracts outstanding active obligations. The accounts view currently falls back to total assets when that endpoint is unavailable, potentially omitting debts while labeling the result net worth; show unavailable instead. Account reconciliation adjustments affect balances but are excluded from spending and income reports. Display differences in liquid, investment and total assets must retain their specific scope.
- Account investment views compute value from market or manual pricing, capital gains against known basis, and parent aggregates. Preserve estimated-valuation and unknown-basis states, audit aggregation and round-trip totals using synthetic holdings, and do not represent estimated valuations as verified live balances.
- Goals report each target's remaining amount, completion percentage and monthly pace based on remaining target and calendar months; this is a user-target planning figure, not an extrapolated prediction. Obligations report remaining principal and payoff progress, with actual ledger allocation changing debt state. The payoff-time estimate divides remaining principal by minimum payment without modeling interest or changes. Remove it from presentation (owner decision 2026-10-30), preserving the underlying balances and payment data.
- Dashboard overview and analytics expose selected-period inflow, outflow, net, category breakdown, reimbursement totals, Kakeibo, comparison and cumulative series; the UI also consumes legacy savings-rate, pace and forecast fields. Net cash-flow divided by inflow is a surplus ratio, not necessarily actual savings. Category budget remaining depends on explicit limits; historical and filtered rows must reconcile to the same scope.
- The existing percent-change helper returns +100% for any positive amount when the previous value is zero. That is not a calculable percentage change; the new display must show a zero-baseline or unavailable state and the underlying amounts. Analytics' weekday `average_spent` is per transaction, not per weekday, and should not be mislabeled as a daily average.
